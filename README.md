# LibTaxiTime-1.0

How long a flight-master flight takes on **WoW: Forever**, and where the one in the air is. An
embedded LibStub library: each addon that uses it ships a copy, and the newest copy loaded runs.

The client gives no flight duration. LibTaxiTime works it out from the length of the path the
taxi flies, taken from the client's own flight-path tables (`Routes.lua`, generated by
`Tools/taxi_routes.py`), at 30 yards a second. Against InFlight's measured Classic flight times,
those lengths give 29.96 yd/s median over 266 routes (standard deviation 0.16), and a route with
stops is the sum of its hops. Once you've landed a route, its own time replaces the prediction.
Frequent Flier (a Legacy perk) makes flights 20% faster.

Used by [Glass Panel](https://github.com/Spotnick2/GlassPanel)'s Flight plugin and Glass Unit
Frames' flight bar.

## Embedding

`.pkgmeta`, at the **main addon's** `Libs/` (never inside a folder `move-folders` moves):

```yaml
externals:
  Libs/LibTaxiTime-1.0:
    url: https://github.com/Spotnick2/LibTaxiTime
    tag: r1
ignore:
  - Libs/LibTaxiTime-1.0/tests
  - Libs/LibTaxiTime-1.0/docs
  - Libs/LibTaxiTime-1.0/Tools
  - Libs/LibTaxiTime-1.0/AGENTS.md
  - Libs/LibTaxiTime-1.0/CLAUDE.md
  - Libs/LibTaxiTime-1.0/README.md
```

TOC, before your own files: `Libs\LibTaxiTime-1.0\LibTaxiTime-1.0.xml`.

## Using it

```lua
local LTT = LibStub("LibTaxiTime-1.0")

-- At your ADDON_LOADED (your SavedVariables are loaded then). The library keeps
-- learned times in `routes` and the flight in the air in `current` of that table.
LTT:AddStore(MyAddonDB.flight)

EventRegistry:RegisterCallback("LibTaxiTime.Changed", function()
    local f = LTT:Current()   -- nil off a flight
    if f then
        -- f.to, f.from, f.stops (names), f.start (GetTime() seconds),
        -- f.total (seconds, or nil: unknown or landing early),
        -- f.source ("learned" | "route" | "estimate" | nil), f.early, f.resumed
    end
end, MyAddon)
```

`LTT:Last()` gives `{ to, took }` for the last flight, `LTT:LearnedCount()` how many routes have
a landed time, and `LTT:Forget()` forgets them. `total` stays the prediction if the flight runs
longer, so draw the overtime yourself.

Nothing runs (no frame, hook or event) until a store is added. With several addons, a landing is
written to every store and a lookup takes the newest entry, so removing one addon loses nothing.
